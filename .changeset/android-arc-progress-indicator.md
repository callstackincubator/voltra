Add `VoltraAndroid.ArcProgressIndicator`, a determinate arc gauge for Android
widgets: a partial ring that fills clockwise, with configurable stroke width,
start and sweep angles, rounded or flat ends, an optional sweep gradient, and
children centered inside the arc. It renders on every supported Android
version, including the versions where Glance's own circular indicator is
indeterminate only.
